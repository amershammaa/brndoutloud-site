03 / How we work

A simple, focused process.

From kickoff to launch in eight to twelve weeks, with one team across every discipline.
01

Discovery.

A working session with the founder to understand the business, the audience and the goal. No template surveys.

02

Strategy.

Positioning, voice and naming if you need it. We finish the strategy before any design work begins.

03

Design and build.

Identity, website and social system, built in parallel by the same two people from start to finish.

04

Launch.

A coordinated rollout across the site, social and paid channels, with day-one content ready to go.
