Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 September 2026

This policy explains how BRND OUT LOUD ("we", "us") handles the information you share with us through our website, our WhatsApp and social media channels, and the enquiry forms in our ads on Facebook and Instagram.

What we collect

  • Your name, phone number (including WhatsApp) and, if you share them, your email and Instagram account.
  • The details you give us about your project: its stage, the service you need and a short description.
  • Messages you send us.

How we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and contact you about your project, by WhatsApp, phone or email.
  • To prepare a proposal and deliver the work you ask for.
  • To understand which of our ads bring relevant enquiries, so we can improve them.

Sharing

We do not sell your information. We only share it with service providers we use to run our business (for example Meta, which provides the ad forms, and our messaging and email tools), and only as needed to contact you and do the work.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details for as long as needed to follow up on your project and for our normal business records, then delete them.

Your choices

You can ask us at any time to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you, or to stop contacting you. Just email hi@brndoutloud.com.
